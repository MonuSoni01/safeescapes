1. Information We Collect

To provide a smooth purchase workflow, handle shipping logistics, and validate product warranties, we collect two categories of information from You:

A. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

This is information You voluntarily provide to us when purchasing a product, creating an account, or registering Your device. It includes:

  • Identification Data: Full Legal Name, Company/Corporate Entity Name, and details of Authorized Signatories.
  • Contact Data: Primary Mobile Number, Alternative Contact Numbers, and Email Addresses.
  • Transaction & Purchase Data: Order ID, Invoice Number, Billing Address, Payment Method details (processed through secure payment gateways), and specific product variants purchased (such as floor selection and wire rope length).
  • Installation & Site Data: Full physical deployment address (including Flat/Room/Suite No., Floor Number, Wing/Block, Street Name, City, State, and Pincode), Installation Date, Name of the Installer, and Structural Mounting Substrate Material.
  • Device Tracing Data: Laser-engraved Product/Device Serial Numbers.
  • Visual Verification Proof: High-resolution photographs uploaded by You showing the installed device serial number and the structural anchor assembly configuration on Your building’s substrate.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When You browse the Website, our servers automatically collect non-personal data, including:

  • Device and Browser Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Geographical Data: Approximate location derived from Your IP address, and precise GPS Geo-Tag Coordinates captured exclusively upon Your explicit permission during the online warranty registration process to anchor the device to a verified high-rise building map location.
  • Usage Logs: Clickstream data, pages viewed, time spent on the Website, and referral sources.

We process Your personal data strictly for legitimate commercial and legal purposes, including:

  • Warranty Validation & Registration: Processing Your mandatory product registration form, verifying structural installation data, tracking the product's strict 5-year operational lifespan, and calculating your official product Expiry Date.

  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Safeguarding Innovative Ideaz Global Private Limited and its Directors from potential legal claims, validating liability waivers, and keeping transactional logs for tax, accounting, and consumer protection audits under Indian law.
  • Unilateral Policy Updates: Informing You when we amend or update our core Terms and Conditions, Shipping Policies, or Privacy Frameworks.

We do not sell, rent, or trade Your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We disclose Your information only to trusted third parties under strict confidentiality clauses for the following operational needs:

  • Logistics and Carrier Plugins (Shiprocket): Your Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and complete physical Shipping Address are automatically synced with Shiprocket and its underlying courier partners (such as Delhivery, BlueDart, DHL) to successfully execute product delivery and automated tracking messages.

Innovative Ideaz Global Private Limited implements robust technical and administrative security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of Your personal data.
