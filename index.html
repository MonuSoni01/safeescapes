The overlooked risk

WHEN FIRE BLOCKS THE STAIRS, WHAT IS YOUR PLAN B?

High-rise evacuation depends on routes remaining usable. Smoke, heat or power loss can change that within minutes. Preparedness starts by planning for the route you hope you never need.

01

Fire brigades take time

Professional rescue may take time to reach the site and upper floors.

02

Water may not reach high floors

Height and access constraints can complicate firefighting.

03

Lifts become unusable

Power failure and fire-safety protocols can make lifts unavailable.

04

Staircases can become dangerous

Fire and smoke can make normal stair routes difficult or unsafe.

05

Upper floors can be isolated

Occupants may have limited conventional evacuation options.

06

Panic can force desperate choices

When no route seems usable, unsafe decisions can follow.

THE REAL CONSEQUENCES

What Can Happen When a Fire Leaves People With No Way Out?

When normal escape routes become unavailable, the consequences can affect lives, families, businesses and responsible parties.

01

Loss of Life

When occupants become trapped by fire, smoke or blocked exits, precious minutes can turn into irreversible loss of life.

02

Panic, Injuries & Fatal Falls

When escape routes seem impossible, people may attempt desperate jumps from upper floors, causing severe injuries or fatalities.

03

Criminal & Legal Consequences

If negligence or safety violations are established, responsible persons may face investigations, prosecution, fines and imprisonment.

04

Heavy Financial Liability

A serious fire can result in compensation claims, property losses, legal expenses and substantial financial liability.

05

Damage to Reputation

News coverage and social media can rapidly turn a fire incident into a major reputational crisis affecting public trust.

06

Business Disruption

A major incident can force evacuation, closure, investigations and operational shutdowns.

07

Questions of Accountability

After a tragedy, management may face difficult questions: Were adequate safety measures in place? Could more have been done?

08

Long-Term Human & Financial Impact

Victims and families may face prolonged treatment, loss of income, emotional trauma and legal consequences.

Protect lives, protect your reputation, and protect your business from the legal and financial consequences of an emergency you never expected.

Introducing SAFE ESCAPE

YOUR BUILDING HAS A FIRE PLAN. DOES IT HAVE AN ESCAPE PLAN?

SAFE ESCAPE is a high-rise emergency evacuation system designed to provide an alternate escape route when stairs or conventional exits become inaccessible during a fire or other emergency. The controlled descent device enables occupants to descend from elevated floors in a controlled manner, providing an additional evacuation option when every second matters.

  • Wire-rope length configured to installation height
