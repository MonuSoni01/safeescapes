Thank you for choosing SAFE ESCAPE. We are committed to supplying genuine, quality-checked emergency evacuation products and ensuring that your order reaches you safely and in good condition.

This Shipping, Delivery, Returns & Replacement Policy explains the terms applicable to purchases made through the SAFE ESCAPE website, including standard products, longer-length configurations, bulk orders and customized/project orders.

1. SHIPPING & DELIVERY

1.1 Order Processing

Orders placed through the SAFE ESCAPE website will normally be processed after:

  • Successful confirmation of the order;
  • Confirmation of payment;
  • Verification of the customer's shipping and contact details.

Orders are generally dispatched within 3–7 business days from payment confirmation, unless a different dispatch timeline is communicated at the time of purchase.

For products requiring additional preparation, customization, longer wire/rope lengths or project-specific configurations, the dispatch timeline may be longer.

The customer will be informed if there is a significant delay in dispatch.

1.2 Delivery Time

After dispatch, delivery time will depend on the destination, courier/logistics partner, availability of transportation and other circumstances beyond SAFE ESCAPE's direct control.

  • Delhi/NCR: approximately 2–5 business days
  • Major Indian cities: approximately 3–7 business days
  • Other locations: approximately 7–10 business days

These are indicative timelines only and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Remote locations, difficult-to-service pin codes, public holidays, extreme weather, strikes, transportation disruptions and other unforeseen circumstances may result in additional delays.

2. SHIPPING CHARGES

We ship FREE all over India. This is a limited time offer and may be discontinued without any prior information/intimation.

For bulk or institutional orders, shipping terms may be agreed separately in the quotation or purchase order.

For international buyers, shipping charges will be extra and will depend on the destination country, shipping method and applicable customs/duties.

3. DELIVERY ADDRESS

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, including:

  • Name of recipient;
  • Building/company name;
  • Flat/office number;
  • Street/locality;
  • City and state;
  • PIN code;
  • Valid mobile number.

SAFE ESCAPE will not be responsible for delays, failed deliveries or additional transportation costs resulting from an incorrect, incomplete or inaccessible address provided by the customer.

4. INSPECTION AT THE TIME OF DELIVERY

Because SAFE ESCAPE is an emergency evacuation product, customers are strongly advised to inspect the package immediately upon delivery.

  • Visible damage to the outer packaging;
  • Broken or opened packaging;
  • Missing components;
  • Damage to the device or accessories;
  • Any visible signs of mishandling during transportation.

Where possible, customers should take photographs/video of the package before opening it, particularly if the package appears damaged.

5. DAMAGED PRODUCTS RECEIVED IN TRANSIT

If the product is damaged during transportation, the customer should notify SAFE ESCAPE within 48 hours of delivery.

The complaint should include:

  1. Order number;
  2. Customer name and contact number;
  3. Photographs of the outer package;
  4. Photographs of the damaged product;
  5. A short description of the damage;
  6. Unboxing video, if available.

6. RETURN POLICY

SAFE ESCAPE products are specialized emergency evacuation equipment and are not ordinary consumer goods. Therefore, returns are subject to applicable conditions.

  • Wrong product supplied;
  • Manufacturing defect;
  • Material damage before delivery;
  • Incorrect fulfilment by SAFE ESCAPE;
  • Return specifically approved by SAFE ESCAPE in writing.

Returns are not automatically accepted simply because the customer has changed their mind after placing the order.

7. PRODUCTS THAT CANNOT NORMALLY BE RETURNED

  • Installed or permanently mounted products;
  • Used or operated products;
  • Modified or dismantled products;
  • Products damaged after delivery;
  • Improperly handled products;
  • Customized configurations;
  • Longer/custom wire or rope configurations;
  • Confirmed bulk/project orders.

8. CUSTOMIZED & LONGER-LENGTH PRODUCTS

Products manufactured according to customer requested length, configuration or project requirement may be considered customized products.

Such products are generally non-returnable and non-cancellable once production or preparation has commenced, except approved manufacturing defects or incorrect supply by SAFE ESCAPE.
